## Projects

### [$nap](https://github.com/lucas304dn/Snap) — 🏆 1st place, bunq × Anthropic × AWS Hackathon 7.0
A social layer built on top of bunq that makes spending memorable. You pay, you have 5 minutes to snap a photo, friends react, guess the amount, and Claude summarises your week. Think BeReal meets Splitwise, powered by multimodal AI.

### [Cortex](https://github.com/jaimereypardo-star/Cortex) - 🏅 Won Top 5 at EurHackNL Hackathon 
A vibe-marketing platform that turns real-time brain activation analysis from your ads into actionable insights, using Meta's open-source TRIBE v2 model. ngrok and FastAPI power the model server backend.

### [Re3ieve](https://github.com/jaimereypardo-star/re3ieve) - 🏅 1st Place at Leiden University’s first official AI team hackathon.

A NotebookLM-style educational study assistant with hybrid semantic and BM25 retrieval, clickable source citations, and multimodal document support (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, images, ZIP). A strict guardrail prevents the model from ever giving direct answers to assignments or quizzes.
